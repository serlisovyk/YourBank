# Lighthouse 100: the font loads early and swaps without a shift; what is left alone

Date: 2026-10-24 · Status: accepted

## Context

Production scored 100 in Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO everywhere, and in Performance on every
cell but Home on a phone (98-99). The one cause that counts was layout shift (CLS up to 0.069): the
hero text wraps differently in the system font until Lexend arrives, and `font-display: swap` moves
it then. Speed Index and TBT in the lab move with the load of the machine that runs it.

## Decision

- **Lexend is preloaded** in `shared/head`, with the source path (`/assets/fonts/Lexend.woff2`): Vite
  rewrites it to the hashed file, as it does in the CSS. The font no longer waits for the stylesheet.
- **A fallback face removes the shift left when the font is late.** `Lexend Fallback` is a local
  `Arial` scaled by `size-adjust` to Lexend's measured average width (107% at weight 300, 111% at
  400-500), after `Lexend` in `--font-family`. `font-display` stays `swap`: every visitor still ends on
  Lexend. A new weight needs its own check of the ratio.
- **Left as they are, though Lighthouse lists them** (none is scored): the LCP is the hero card's CSS
  background decor, so the request starts after the stylesheet (a `preload` per page needs a data field
  and gained about 70 ms, no score); the stylesheet blocks rendering for 150-440 ms on a throttled phone
  (9 kB over the wire; inlining it needs a build plugin and a heavier page); the page photos are
  larger than their phone size (they are stand-ins); the forced reflow of
  `Reveal` (it must read positions before it hides anything, `reveal-on-scroll`).

## Consequences

- A change that shifts the first screen is measured again, with the font held back, not only on a warm cache.
- When the real photos replace the stand-ins they get a `srcset` sized to the phone and the desktop, and
  the preload of a first-screen image is reconsidered then.
- A score of 98-99 on Home mobile from a busy machine, with CLS 0, is lab noise, not a regression.
- Rejected: `font-display: optional` (a first visit would show the system font); `swap` alone, even
  with the preload (the swap still lands after the first paint on a slow link, and the shift returns).
