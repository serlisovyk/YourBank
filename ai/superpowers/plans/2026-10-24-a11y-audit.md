# Plan: accessibility audit (MY-204)

Goal: the accessibility audit of the four pages and `404.html` (a copy of Home), and the fixes it
finds. Behavior is in `ai/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-a11y-audit.md`. Runs beside the design
fidelity task (MY-210): this one does not touch the `section-heading` text width, the FAQ fade, the
`page-hero` or `job-card` height.

## Author's decisions

- The Values titles keep the faded look, but reach 3:1 (large text): a token, not a hex in CSS.
- Touch targets keep the drawn size from the design; the hit area grows to 44 px, invisibly.

## Audit method

Headless Chrome over the DevTools protocol on `npm run build` + `npm run preview`; nothing is added
to `package.json`.

- axe-core 4.10.2, injected into the page, on `/`, `/careers`, `/about`, `/security`, `/404.html` at
  1920 and 390 px, before and after. The body's dotted background image makes axe skip every colour
  contrast check ("incomplete"), so contrast is also computed by script against the solid background.
- A width sweep (320 to 1920 px) for horizontal overflow; a scan of every control for its size.
- Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, Escape and the arrows sent as key events: tab order and ring, the
  menu, the Products toggle, the Features tabs, the converter, the carousel, the FAQ, Apply Now, the CTA.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` emulated; computed durations and `scroll-behavior` read.
- The accessibility tree (`Accessibility.getFullAXTree`) of the converter and the carousel.

## Changes

- `shared/header`: a "Skip to content" link first in the header (a `.button`, positioned by
  `.header__skip-link`, hidden until focus); `<main id="main" tabindex="-1">` on the four pages;
  `main:focus-visible` carries no ring: it is a target, not a control. The menu button moves before the
  menu in the markup, so Tab from the open button enters the menu (the CSS grid places both).
- `variables.css`: `--touch-target` (44px) and `--grey-45`; `values.css` takes `--grey-45`.
- `ui/hit-area.css`: a utility, like `visually-hidden`: `position: relative` and an `::after` that
  grows the box to `--touch-target` on each axis where it is smaller, and not at all where it is not.
  Used on the menu button, the toggle buttons, the menu links and the two logos. The menu list keeps
  2 px of padding (cancelled by a negative margin) so its `overflow: hidden` does not clip the area.
- Home hero: `h3` "Your Transactions" becomes a `p`; "Money Exchange" becomes an `h2` (a working
  tool next to the `h1`, the group's name stays on it); `hero.css` / `currency-converter.css` keep
  the same type.
- `hero.css`: the Supported Currency plaque keeps 60 px outside the container where the gutter
  allows, less where it does not; `case-item.css`: columns `minmax(0, 1fr)` and hyphenated titles.
- `testimonial-carousel`: `tabindex="0"`, `role="group"` and a label on the track; the edge mask is off
  while it has focus, so the ring is whole.
- `reset.css`: the reduced-motion reset also covers `::before` and `::after` (the FAQ fade ran on).
- Anything else the audit finds serious or critical.
