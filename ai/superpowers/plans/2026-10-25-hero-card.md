# Plan: the hero card as drawn (MY-212)

Goal: the right-hand block of the Home hero matches the design, the converter's native drop-down is
dark, and the card no longer changes height after load. No spec: the converter behaves as before;
only the look, the build-time markup and the list colours change. Decisions kept:
`fixed-rates-native-select` (a native select, fixed rates), `reveal-on-scroll` (never the first
screen), `touch-targets-hit-area`, `breakpoint-scheme` (fluid tokens, two structural queries).

## Measured against the design (1920, card 515 wide)

- Card: flat `--grey-10` fill (hides the arrows behind it), 1 px border `--grey-15` that turns olive
  at the top-right and bottom-left corners; no `min-height`, the content sets it (about 624).
  Padding 34. The arrows are no longer a background of `.hero__inner`: they sit behind the card and
  stick out of it on the right by about 45 % of their width.
- Transactions: title 18, 17 below it the stack. Row 1 is a full card (75 high, padding 12 × 20,
  `--grey-11` fill); rows 2 and 3 sit 53 lower, 17 narrower on each side per step, behind the one above,
  at 50 % / 20 % opacity; a row covers the top of the next. Texts: label 16, name 18, amount 20.
  The amount is `-$68.00` without a space.
- Money Exchange: title 18, 24 below it the box (179 high, `--grey-11` fill, `--grey-15` dividers):
  the head is the flag, the code (16), then the name on one line (14); the amount cell is 80 high.
  Exchange button 16 / 52 high, 24 under the box.
- Pills: `+ $5000,00` (18) and `Monthly Income` (14) in a 188 × 75 pill, 60 left of the card and 44
  above it; `Supported Currency` (16) starts 13 below the card and sticks out 52 on the right.
- 1440 and 390 are not drawn for this block: the values above scale with the card (410 at 1440, 305 at 390) through tokens; Home at 390 keeps the stacked, centred layout.

## Changes

- `variables.css`: `--green-20` (the olive of the border), `--hero-unit` (card width / 515) and two
  type tokens that scale with it; `--hero-card-height` goes.
- `hero.css`, `currency-converter.css`: the stack, the sizes and the border above; arrows on
  `.hero__content::before`; `overflow-x: clip` on the hero, so the arrows never scroll the page.
- `hero.html`: the rows keep their loop; the stack comes from CSS. `hero-transactions.js`: `-$68.00`.
- The select: `option` gets its background and colour from tokens, so the list is dark in Chrome on
  Windows; the select keeps `color-scheme: dark`.
- No height jump: a new data file `converter.js` (`converter`: the default From and To, the amount and
  the result worked out from `currency-rates.js` with the script's own constants) is wired into
  `context`; the partial renders the default flags, names and codes, the selected options and the
  result, and only the other currencies' markers stay `hidden`. `CurrencyConverter` is unchanged.
- The hero card has no `data-reveal` and no lazy image today: checked, nothing to remove.
