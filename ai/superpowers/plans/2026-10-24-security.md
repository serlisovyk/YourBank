# Plan: security (MY-201)

Goal: the Security page: Hero → How We Protect You → FAQ → footer (no CTA). No spec: the page is new and
only reuses existing patterns (`page-hero`, `icon-card`, the shared FAQ). No open questions.

## Changes

- Hero: `{{> sections/shared/page-hero page.hero }}` with a `hero` entry in `pages.security`
  (`titleStart` "Your Security is Our", `titleAccent` "Top Priority", `text`, `image`). `page-hero`
  and its stylesheet are not touched, so Careers renders as before.
- Hero photo: `assets/images/security-hero.webp`, 968 × 716 (the Careers size), `alt=""`. **A stand-in:**
  cropped from a small design reference and upscaled, so it is blurry; the author replaces it with a
  same-named export, no code change. The part under the text card is filled with a blurred mirror
  of its neighbour (the card hides it above 1024 px). About 10 kB.
- Section `partials/sections/security/protection.html`: `section-heading` ("How We" + "Protect You" and
  the intro), then `.protection__items`: `protection` from `data/protection.js` as `components/icon-card`
  with `inset=true` (built for it). Wired in `vite.config.js` (`context`).
- Backdrop: `.protection__items` itself — a 2-column grid (`--space-grid-gap`), padded by the fluid token
  `--space-protection` (95 at 1920, 80 at 1440, 40 at 768), over the existing `dots.avif` at its top left
  on `--grey-10` and radius `--border-radius`. The backdrop is the section's style, not the card's. Below
  768 px: one column, no padding, no backdrop (the design stacks bare cards). No new decor raster.
- Icons `icons/secure-platform.svg`, `multi-factor.svg`, `fraud-monitoring.svg`, `mobile-banking.svg`: the
  circle-and-ring structure of `compensation.svg`, a new filled `#caff33` glyph each (apps with a plus,
  layers, sparkles, a coin), 1.1–1.3 kB.
- `styles/sections/protection.css`, imported in `style.css` after `benefits.css`.
- `security.html`: hero, protection, `{{> sections/shared/faq name="security" groups=questionGroups }}`
  (the shared set: the design shows the same questions as Home).
