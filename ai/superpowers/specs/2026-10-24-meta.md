# Spec: meta and link previews (MY-202)

What a visitor, a crawler and a chat app see. The plan is `ai/superpowers/plans/2026-10-24-meta.md`.

## Behaviour

- Every page has its own `<title>` and meta `description`; no two pages share either.
- Every page names its canonical URL on the production origin, a clean URL: `/`, `/careers`, `/about`,
  `/security` (no `.html`, no trailing slash except the root).
- The browser tab shows the YourBank mark (SVG favicon); iOS gets a 180×180 icon; the address bar
  and the mobile browser chrome take `#1A1A1A`.
- A link to any page pasted in a chat or a social network unfurls into a large card: the page's title
  and description, the site name `YourBank`, and one preview image (1200×630, the Home first screen)
  with a text alternative.
- The tags are Open Graph (`og:site_name`, `title`, `description`, `url`, `type=website`, `image`,
  its size and `alt`) and Twitter (`summary_large_image`, `title`, `description`, `image`). Image
  URLs are absolute.
- `/404` answers with the Home page, so its canonical and `og:url` are Home's.
- Nothing visible on the pages changes.

## Not in scope

- A web app manifest, `favicon.ico`, per-page preview images.
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, structured data.
