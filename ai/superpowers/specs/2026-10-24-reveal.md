# Spec: reveal on scroll (MY-205)

## Behavior

- Below the first screen, the section headings and the cards, rows and items of every page fade in
  and rise a little when they scroll into view: opacity 0 to 1 and a 20 px move up, 0.5 s, once.
  An element that has appeared never hides again, however the page is scrolled.
- What reveals: every section heading (title, text, toggle); Home: the product cards and their
  dividers, the use-case rows, the feature items, the FAQ items, the testimonials carousel, the CTA;
  Careers: the values, the benefit cards, the job cards, the FAQ items, the CTA; About: the Mission
  and Vision rows and the press cards; Security: the protection cards and the FAQ items.
- Elements that enter together cascade: the second starts 0.08 s after the first, and so on; the
  cascade stops growing at the fifth element (0.32 s).
- The first screen never animates: the Home hero and the page hero of Careers, About and Security
  are drawn at once and so is anything already in view when the page opens, however tall the window.
  A page opened at an anchor or at a scrolled position shows what is in view without a fade.
- A hidden tab or panel (Products and Testimonials audience, Features category, the rest of the FAQ)
  reveals its elements the first time it is shown.
- The header and the footer do not animate.

## Without the animation

- Without JavaScript, or when the script fails, nothing is hidden: the page is complete.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the script hides nothing, so everything is visible at once and
  nothing moves.

## Keyboard and assistive technology

- Tab never lands on an invisible element: focus inside a hidden element shows it at once, with no
  fade, and it stays shown afterwards.
- The text is in the page and in the reading order the whole time; only the look changes, so a
  screen reader reads it the same with or without the animation.

## Layout

- Nothing is resized or moved in the flow: only `opacity` and `transform`, so the page does not
  shift (CLS unchanged) and there is no horizontal scroll from the 20 px offset.

## Not changed

- The hero, the header, the footer, the Disclosure, the Carousel and the Toggle behavior.
