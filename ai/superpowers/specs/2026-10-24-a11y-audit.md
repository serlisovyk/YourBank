# Spec: accessibility audit (MY-204)

## Behavior

- Every page, `dist/404.html` included, starts its tab order with a "Skip to content" link. It is
  invisible until it takes focus, then shows as a green button at the top left and, on Enter,
  moves focus to `<main>` so the next Tab lands on the first control of the page.
- The mobile menu button, the Individuals / Businesses buttons, the menu links and the logos keep
  their drawn size; their hit area is at least 44 px high (the width where it is under 44 px too).
- The testimonial track is a tab stop of its own, with the same focus ring; the arrow keys scroll it.
- The Home hero has one `h1`; "Your Transactions" is plain text, "Money Exchange" is an `h2`.
  No page skips a heading level.
- The Values titles on Careers are lighter grey: 3:1 or more on the page background, still the
  faded look.
- Nothing scrolls the page sideways from 320 px up: not the Supported Currency plaque at
  1024 to 1112 px, not the Use Cases icons at 320 to 330 px.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` no transition, animation or smooth scroll runs.

## Not changed

- Layout and look at 1920, 1440 and 390 px, apart from the Values title colour.
- Texts, the pages' structure, the scripts' behavior.
